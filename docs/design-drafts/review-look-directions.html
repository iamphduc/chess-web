Post-game review: three looks

A design draft for the review that opens after a vs-Liem game. Each direction keeps the app's palette, Quicksand and the Wheat board, and adds only grade colors. Pick a direction with the tabs. Each one shows, in order: a one-line thesis and its named memorable detail, palette, type scale, every control in every state (static copies: hover, pressed and focus are drawn, not live), the review's own components, then one screen at 1200 px and one at 375 px. Wide screens scroll sideways inside their frame.

Sample game: you (White) lose to Liem at 2100 in a Sicilian Najdorf, English Attack, 0–1 in 38 moves. It left his book at move 13. Key moments are your three largest win-% drops: 13.Nd5? (−22), 20.Bd3? (−23), 27.Qe2?? (−44). Every board is the real position from this game.

Coach

A coach walks you through three moments, one card at a time, and the rest of the review stays quiet behind it.

Memorable detail: “Gold stars for found moves.” Each key moment has an empty star, the same gold star that sits next to his name. Find the move yourself and it lights up. The lesson ends on “2 of 3 found.”
