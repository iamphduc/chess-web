At a glance

Goal. A second polish round on the screens ui-polish shipped, from the human's own look at them. In the human's words: "+4 feel like reversed", "the end game notice feels too plain for me", "the piece movement is quite slow, and the playback buttons doesn't play piece movement", and "It should be the "Play again" button and remove that from the navbar".

What shipped.

  • Each player card has a second line under the name: the captured pieces, then "+N" (for example ♛♜♝♞♞♟ +4).
  • The game-over card waits 300 ms so you see the last move, then the scrim fades in and the card rises into place. With reduced motion it shows at once.
  • Pieces move in 150 ms with an ease-out instead of Framer Motion's default spring. A drag drop lands at once.
  • In the review, ◀, ▶ and the arrow keys slide the one move. A jump (a far move, First, Last, a key moment) snaps.
  • The review's Moves card foot has Play again instead of Back to game. The top bar has only ← Home and the title.

Key decisions

All entries are in pod-docs/decisions.md, dated 2026-10-20. Design draft: none ("the motion can't be judged from a still page", the plan's Look).

Pieces first, then "+N", on a line under the name

Why
The human found "+4" before the glyphs "reversed".
Turned down
The 2026-10-19 layout: "+N", then glyphs, on the name line.
Consequence
Two lines in a 56 px card; card padding went from --space-2 to --space-1.
Who
human accepted the recommendation

Entry: "The material line sits under the name, pieces then +N"

His detail shares the material line, material first

Why
Three lines don't fit 56 px. Material first means neither moves when the other comes or goes.
Turned down
Detail first, which "makes the material jump about 70 px each time "Thinking…" comes and goes".
Consequence
Queued for the human; the value is in use.
Who
agent sprint-planner default, override anytime

Entry: "His card's detail shares the material line, material first"

The name stays put on a card with no captures

Why
The second line's space is always kept, so the name never moves at the first capture.
Turned down
Center the name until the first capture, then move it up once.
Consequence
On "You", White and Black at the start, the name sits about 12 px above the disc's middle.
Who
agent M1 engineer default, override anytime

Entry: "The name stays put on a card with no captures"

A subtle game-over entrance after a short pause

Why
The notice felt "too plain"; the pause lets the mating move land first.
Turned down
A bounce, and a king highlight.
Consequence
Once per game end; instant under reduced motion and on a later mount; New game during the pause cancels it.
Who
human chose option a and the values

Entry: "The game-over card eases in after a short pause"

Pieces move in 150 ms with an ease-out

Why
Movement felt "quite slow" with the default spring.
Turned down
Framer Motion's default spring.
Consequence
One constant for play and review. A drag drop now lands at once; castling's rook jumps with it on a drop.
Who
human speed; agent the drop rule (P1)

Entry: "Pieces move in 150 ms with an ease-out"

The review animates one-step moves only; jumps snap

Why
Pieces keep their ids across positions, so a jump would sweep every piece across the board.
Turned down
Not recorded; the risk the plan names is a jump that slides every piece.
Consequence
Review pieces get their own layout ids (review-…), and each piece its own element.
Who
human accepted the recommendation

Entry: "The review animates one-step moves only; jumps snap"

Play again in the review's foot, none in the top bar

Why
The human: "What is the point of "Back to game" button?"
Turned down
The 2026-10-19 default: Play again in the top bar, Back to game in the foot (now superseded).
Consequence
Back to the finished game is the browser's Back. TopBar's action prop stays, unused.
Who
human

Entry: "Play again sits in the review's Moves card foot"

Ships in ui-polish-staging

Why
So it reaches main together with PR #93.
Turned down
Not recorded.
Consequence
#98 targets ui-polish-staging, not main.
Who
human accepted

Inside wave 1

The three slices ran side by side and each wrote its tests first. G1 noted one test, "reduced motion shows it at once never went red against the stub". P1's browser check found that ◀ after a capture reused the taker's element for the piece it took, so Framer faded the returning piece out. P1 fixed it in the slice by keying each review piece by its type (db87a25, a0dae42). M1 lost about 10 minutes to chrome-devtools-axi eval quoting on Windows; the orchestrator wrote it up as pod-docs/known-issues/chrome-devtools-axi-eval-quoting.md.

Review and fix

Round 1's one FIX: at 375 px the glyph row never cut. .player__material had min-width: auto, so it couldn't shrink, and "+N" and his detail slid under the clock; with 15 glyphs the page scrolled sideways (scrollWidth 473). The CSS test checked the children's rules but not the wrapper's. The fix engineer added that check, saw it go red, and added min-width: 0 with no overflow, so "+N" isn't clipped. Round 2 re-ran the repro at 375 and 320 px, saw the new assertion fail on 086bea6 and pass on 444f0f5, and passed the PR.

Where the human stepped in: the plan interview (the four complaints and the acceptance of every recommendation), and the choice to ship into ui-polish-staging. No human answer was recorded during the run. The two agent defaults (his detail's place, the name staying put) wait for the human in handoff-queue.md and decisions.md.
