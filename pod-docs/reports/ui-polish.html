At a glance

Goal. Polish the screens that ui-refresh shipped, from the human's first look at them. The page stops jumping (scroll and clocks), the board cards say less, you can resign, the end of a game is clear, and the review sidebar drops from five cards to three.

What shipped.

  • A move never scrolls the page. There were three causes, found one after another (see the timeline).
  • Body text is Quicksand 600 (bold stays 700). The clock is a fixed 112 px box, and each digit sits in its own fixed cell.
  • Your card shows a ♔ or ♚ disc. His card shows only the level name, "★ Book" while he's in his book, "Thinking…" and "+N". The out-of-book note is gone. In Two players, the cards read White and Black.
  • Resign in the Moves card foot, with a red Confirm second press within 3 s, at the same 104 px width.
  • At the end of a game, the board dims under a card with the result, how it ended, Review game and New game.
  • The review sidebar is three cards: key moment, summary (graph and one accuracy line), Moves (with a "Left his book" line).

Key decisions

Each card links to its entry in pod-docs/decisions.md (the 2026-10-20 entries). The design draft is pod-docs/design-drafts/ui-polish.html. "Default, accepted" means an agent proposed it in the plan interview and the human accepted it.

Body text is Quicksand 600

Why
500 "looks very thin like a paper which is not fit for a game website".
Alternatives
The other options in draft section 0; the human said "Let's do B".
Consequence
Only the 600 and 700 files ship; the 500 file is gone.
Who
human · "Body text is Quicksand 600" · draft §0

Resign in the foot, with a 3 s red Confirm

Why
"I need a resign button". A second press stops a slip.
Alternatives
A longer "Confirm resign" label, turned down because it "made the button grow onto two lines".
Consequence
A fixed 104 px button; both modes; in Two players only once the clocks run, and Play hides then.
Who
default, accepted; the width and short label human · "Resign sits in the Moves card foot…" · draft §2

The game-over card dims the board

Why
"dim the board to make the endgame more prominent".
Alternatives
The 2026-10-19 cream card with no scrim (now superseded).
Consequence
A 40 % ink scrim that takes no clicks; the card waits for a pending promotion pick.
Who
dim: human; card content: default · "The game-over card dims the board" · draft §3

A color disc in place of a photo

Why
The cartoon picture "is not match our design", and his card already had a ♞ disc.
Alternatives
Keeping chess-player.png.
Consequence
One 40 px disc style for every card; only the Liem step has a photo.
Who
default, accepted · "A color disc in place of a photo" · draft §1

His card shows only his book or Thinking…; no out-of-book note

Why
"Plays Liem's openings or Stockfish … looks busy"; "once we are out of the opening move we will know that the bot is playing".
Alternatives
The 2026-10-18 card lines and the Stockfish pill (superseded).
Consequence
In a game, nothing says Stockfish plays; the home page's engine line still does.
Who
default, accepted; fixed clock width human · "His card shows only…", "No out-of-book note"

The review sidebar is three cards

Why
"too much review cards on the right side … more simple and compact", and the key moment above the graph.
Alternatives
Five cards: graph, key moment, accuracy, Moves, "Left his book".
Consequence
AccuracyPanel and the left-book card are gone; their content is one line each.
Who
order and fewer cards: human; the merge: default · "The review sidebar is three cards" · draft §4

Two players cards read White and Black

Why
The board named its sides "Le Quang Liem" (with his photo) and "Me".
Alternatives
Keep the old names (the 2026-10-19 entry, superseded).
Consequence
The clock labels read "Clock for White" and "Clock for Black".
Who
agent default from the sprint contract · "Two players cards read White and Black"

A move scrolls only the move list

Why
"every time I make a move the scroll bar automatically scrolls to the top". scrollIntoView scrolled every scrollable parent, the page included.
Alternatives
Keep scrollIntoView with options; the contract chose a pure listScrollTop on the box's own scrollTop.
Consequence
The list scroll is instant now. body { overflow-anchor: none } stops Chrome's scroll anchoring too.
Who
a bug, fixed first: human; the method: sprint contract · sprint doc, S1 (no decisions.md entry)

The book note keeps its space on phones

Why
Below 992 px the note sits above the Moves card. When it went, the page got shorter and scrollY dropped (review round 1).
Alternatives
Move the note under the Moves card on phones; that changes the ui-refresh order and still shortens the page.
Consequence
An empty gap above the Moves card for the rest of the game.
Who
agent default, open for the human · "The book note's space stays on phones…"

Clock digits in fixed cells

Why
The self-hosted Quicksand has no tabular figures, so tabular-nums did nothing ("1" is 0.389 em, "0" 0.614 em at 700).
Alternatives
A font file that has tnum.
Consequence
Each digit is a 0.62 em centred cell, each : or . a 0.28 em cell; a test reads the real widths from the woff2.
Who
review fix (#92) · no decisions.md entry; the clock width is in "His card shows only…"

Review and fix pass

  • Round 1 (08:17 UTC): "Verdict: fix". Build and 1,193 tests passed, but two problems showed in the browser:
    • At 375 px, when his reply left his book, the note unmounted. The page "drops from 1055 to 813 px, and scrollY goes 207 to 1." This was the third scroll cause: overflow-anchor: none meant nothing made up for the lost height. "The S1 tests and the manual runs missed this because they never had the note go away."
    • The clock digits still shifted: "font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums does nothing in Quicksand". The reviewer called the CSS test "hollow on this point".
    The reviewer dismissed five other leads, including resign racing his reply ("His move never landed (4 s wait), and the clocks froze").
  • Fix pass (#92, 08:36 UTC): the book note keeps an empty slot as tall as it was, until a new game. It never gets shorter within a game. From 992 px the slot is hidden. Each clock character got its own fixed cell, and tests/clock-digits.test.ts reads the real glyph widths from the woff2.
  • Round 2 (08:43 UTC): "Verdict: pass". Both repros fixed. One PENDING for the human: "from 992 px the Moves card jumps up by the note's height: 242 px at 1400x560", with scrollY unchanged. The reviewer said: "Not a FIX: product choice."
