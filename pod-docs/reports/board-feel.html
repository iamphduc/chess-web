1. At a glance

Goal (from the plan): "Make the board feel like chess.com: restyled move highlights, click-to-move that behaves as players expect, a flip-board button, move sounds with a mute toggle, play that works on a phone, and less motion when the OS asks for it. The board stays custom; only its feel changes."

What shipped:

  • Look A highlights: one yellow tint for the last move and the selected square, grey dots and capture rings, a red glow on the checked king's square, and a white inner edge on a legal drag-over.
  • Click-to-move that deselects and reselects as players expect, and tap-to-move on touch screens.
  • A Flip button that turns the board, the coordinates, the player cards and the clocks.
  • Six CC0 move sounds (Kenney "Impact Sounds") with a Sound toggle that survives a reload.
  • Touch drag through react-dnd-multi-backend, and no piece sliding when the OS asks for reduced motion.

3. Key decisions

docs/decisions.md has no entries for this plan (its newest entry is dated 2026-10-05). The decisions below come from the plan's Key decisions and Look sections, the sprint contracts and the PR bodies. The plan marks agent-chosen defaults with "(default)". It doesn't record who proposed the other plan decisions in the plan interview (Grilled-with: grilling (fast)).

Look direction A (chess.com classic)

human

Why: one yellow signal for "this moved" and "this is in your hand"; the human's pick was "I will pick A".

Alternatives: the other directions in the Look draft.

Consequence: 8 --board-* tokens; green and red drag overlays and the king-piece glow are gone.

Look draft · plan → Look

Keep the custom board

plan

Why: most features already existed, and a switch means a rewrite. Chessground is GPL-3.0 and the repo has no licence yet.

Alternatives: chessground, react-chessboard.

Consequence: every change is local to src/features/board/.

research

Flip only at render time

plan + sprint planner

Why: the board already holds a lot of y/x logic. Redux, the engine and every dispatched [y, x] stay in White's view.

Alternatives: flipping the stored board.

Consequence: one pure helper, toDisplay, is its own inverse; data-square selectors work flipped.

CC0 sounds, not lichess's

plan · pack picked by engineer S1

Why: the lichess sets are AGPL or CC BY-NC-SA, and the standard set has no check sound.

Alternatives: lichess sounds; tones.

Consequence: Kenney "Impact Sounds", converted from .ogg to .wav (9–65 KB), credited in public/sounds/CREDITS.md.

No AudioContext before the first gesture

plan + sprint planner

Why: Chrome's autoplay policy (research → Best practices).

Alternatives: create the context on load.

Consequence: the player retries resume() on each gesture until it runs; no console warnings on load.

One sound per move, by priority

default

Why: a move that checks and captures should play one clear sound.

Rule: game end > check > promotion > castle > capture > move.

Consequence: a pure pickSound, tested on all 15 pairs.

Tap first, then touch drag only if it fits

plan · verdict by engineer T1

Why: research couldn't confirm how the HTML5 backend behaves on touch.

Alternatives: ship tap-to-move only and log PENDING.

Consequence: T1's check said "Verdict: fits", so sprint 2 added the multi-backend, the plan's one allowed new runtime dependency.

touch-drag research

Accept chess.com dot contrast

default

Why: to match chess.com; the Look draft shows the dots are visible on Wheat.

Trade-off: rgba(0,0,0,0.14) is below the WCAG 1.4.11 3:1 ratio.

Smaller defaults

default

Flip is a manual button only, and its state lasts until reload. Sound is on by default. The flip doesn't slide pieces. The board's console.log calls stay. A data-last-sound marker on <html> ships in production for smoke runs (S1 NOTE).
