★Human-style strength for the Liem opponent

Plan human-style-strength · sprint human-moves · merge target main · report written 2026-10-12, before the final merge

After his opening book, the Liem opponent no longer plays Stockfish's weakened skill mode. Stockfish 18 at full strength lists its top 4 moves with scores, and a per-step chooser picks one, so each step makes sized mistakes instead of random blunders. The app now says plainly that Stockfish plays once he leaves his book.

Seven strength steps, each now labelled "about N": 1400, 1700, 1900, 2100, 2300, 2500 and Full (2732).

At a glance

Goal (from the plan): "Make each strength step play like a person at that level, not like Stockfish with random noise." The trigger: the human played the 1400 step and said it "doesn't play like 1400".

What shipped:

  • The engine adapter asks Stockfish for 4 scored lines (MultiPV) at a set depth, with a time limit as a backstop. It never sends UCI_LimitStrength or UCI_Elo.
  • chooseHumanMove: a pure function that scores lines in win %, plays seen mates, avoids walking into mate, never hangs material for nothing, plays tighter when well ahead, and otherwise makes a weighted pick with rare sized slips.
  • A per-step table, STEP_PROFILES, set from four runs of a new dev-only npm run calibrate script.
  • Disclosure: a setup card line, a grey "Stockfish" pill on his card, new out-of-book note text, and a README section.

The plan's first success goal is in doubt. It asked that at 1400 "he makes club-player mistakes (inaccurate moves, rare bigger slips)". The reviewer found the 1400 step "rarely makes club-player mistakes": a 0.0 % slip rate in its own run, and 13 of 511 self-play moves dropping 200 cp or more. In calibration, 1400 beat Stockfish's UCI_Elo 2400 in 3 of 4 games. The odd blunders are gone, but the step may now feel well above 1400. That call is the human's.

Key decisions

A red edge marks a decision the human made or agreed to. In the plan interview the human said "go with all your recommendations", so every plan "(default)" is counted as agreed. Links go to pod-docs/decisions.md by entry title.

Pick among Stockfish's top lines, not UCI_Elo

Why: the research reads Stockfish's source and finds that at UCI_Elo 1400 skill mode picks at depth 1 with a large random push (not tested).

Alternatives: keep skill mode; Maia-style nets; a model trained on his games (out of scope).

Consequence: bestMove became analyse; stronger steps reply slower, up to each step's movetimeMs.

Planner from research; human approved the plan. Entry: "He picks among Stockfish's top lines instead of using UCI_Elo", which replaces part of the 2026-10-09 Stockfish entry. Research.

Compare moves in win %, not centipawns

Why: a 100 cp loss costs little when far ahead and a lot at equality. Lichess formula: 100 cp ≈ 59 %, 300 cp ≈ 75 %.

Alternatives: centipawn gaps, as in the DEV post the research found.

Consequence: win % flattens near won and lost positions, which led to two later fixes and notes (below).

Plan Key decision, from the research. No separate decisions entry.

Rare sized slips, never material for nothing

Why: real 1400s make mistakes; the strange ones the human saw are the ones to remove.

Alternatives: never slip; blunders only in time trouble.

Consequence: hangsMaterial looks one exchange deep (a lone pawn is allowed, a piece is not) and holds even on a slip.

Plan default, agreed by the human.

Depth per step, 4 lines, a time backstop

Why: low steps should miss deep tactics like a person, and phones must stay quick. MultiPV is slow in single-thread WASM, so keep it at 4.

Alternatives: fixed movetime; node limits; 3 or 6 lines.

Consequence: the reviewer found Full never reaches depth 18 in 1000 ms (it stops at 13–16), so for Full the time limit is the real limit.

Plan default, agreed by the human.

Step table from calibration

Why: no source maps loss limits to ratings. The runs moved 1400 from depth 5 to 8 and its slip limit from 25 to 15 points.

Alternatives: keep H1's guesses; tune only by hand.

Consequence: the steps rise against each other, but UCI_Elo anchors "are not on a human scale", so labels stay "about N".

Agent default (K1). Entry: "Strength step table set from the first calibration runs".

Tighter "well ahead" limits at every step

Why: in a play check at 1400 and about +7, he skipped a free queen, worth only about 3 win % points there.

Alternatives: a chooser rule such as "never skip a free capture of 3 or more" (queued as Fix next).

Consequence: 1400's aheadMaxLoss fell from 4 to 2.

Agent default (K1), commit 89a803e. Recorded in the calibration entry.

Say when Stockfish plays

Why: the human wanted it clear the opponent isn't Liem after the opening. Lichess bot guidance asks bots to state their engine.

Alternatives: a per-move badge in the notation (ruled out by the Look); docs only.

Consequence: setup line, grey pill, note text and README; gold stays for his book.

Human request; placement is a plan default. Look reused from play-liem-look.html.

Phone text: "~2100", "★ Book", a 14 px line

Why: on a 312 px board his name column is 136 px, so the line was cut to "GM · …"; at 16 px the setup card ran 38 px past the board.

Alternatives: shrink the avatar, clock or card height.

Consequence: phones lose the book count on his card (the note below still has it).

Agent defaults (W2-fix, U1). Entries: "On phones, his card reads "~2100" and "★ Book"" and "The setup card's Stockfish line is 14 px on phones".

Keep the calibrated 1400, even if it feels strong

Why: it removes the blunders; how a 1400 should feel is the human's call from play.

Alternatives: lower 1400's depth, or raise its maxLoss, temperature or slipChance.

Consequence: 1400 may play above its label until the human tunes it.

Agent default, written after review (uncommitted in the working tree). Entry: "The 1400 step may now feel stronger than 1400".

Where something happened

Wave 2 fix. At 375 px his card line was cut to "GM · …" beside the new pill, and the pill was 2 px taller than the badge. A wave fix (human-moves-w2-fix) shortened the phone line to "~2100" and "★ Book" and set both to 16.8 px. PR #66 then reports "defects: none after the wave fix". It cost about 10 minutes measuring the 136 px column.

Calibration changed the plan's guesses. Run 1 showed 1400's largest slip lost 24 win % points (about a piece). K1 deepened the low steps and narrowed slips. Then a play check caught him skipping a free queen at +7, so K1 cut the ahead limits and ran a fourth, ladder-only run. The Sprint summary notes "one quiet stretch of about 10 min during a run (not a stall)".

No halts. The queue has no BLOCKED entry for this plan. PRs #65–#67 were each merged under the iamphduc account about a minute after they opened; the records don't say whether the human or autopilot ran the merge.
