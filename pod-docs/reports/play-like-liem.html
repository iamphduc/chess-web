★Play like Liem

Plan report for play-like-liem, merge target main. Final PR #63, review verdict pass in round 1.

You can now play a computer opponent that opens like GM Le Quang Liem (FIDE 12401137, 2732). He plays moves from a book built from 3,692 of his Chess.com games. When the game leaves his book, Stockfish takes over at the strength you pick, from 1400 up to his full rating.

Seven strength steps: 1400, 1700, 1900, 2100, 2300, 2500 and full (2732).

At a glance

Goal (from the plan): a computer opponent that plays like Liem. It plays his real openings from a book of his games, then a strength-limited Stockfish takes over. You pick your color and his strength, and a book panel shows what he played in this position. The plan's reason: at full 2700 strength a club player learns little, so his strength can be set, while his openings stay real at every level.

What shipped:

  • A "vs Liem" / "Two players" switch. Two-player mode works as before, and the page still opens in it.
  • A setup card over the dimmed board: color (White, Black or Random), seven strength steps, and Start game.
  • His moves: from his book while the position is in it, else from Stockfish lite (WebAssembly, one thread, in a Web Worker). Every move is checked against the app's own legalMoves.
  • A book note and a "Book" badge that show his move counts, then turn into "Out of his book".
  • A Node import script (npm run book:import) and two committed books that hold only move counts.
  • A GPL-3.0-or-later LICENSE, README credits, self-hosted Quicksand, and a darker app green.

Key decisions

A red edge means the human made or changed the call. An olive edge means an agent or the plan's defaults made it. Entries are in docs/decisions.md under the heading named on each card. The look draft is docs/design-drafts/play-liem-look.html.

Book first, Stockfish after

Why: cloning a 2700 player isn't proven. Personal Maia models needed about 5,000 games, and GM attempts were "less successful" (research).

Instead of: a personal neural model or Maia.

So: openings are really his, and the middlegame is a strength-limited engine.

Planner, from docs/research/play-like-player.md. In the plan, not in decisions.md.

Stockfish lite, one thread, in a Web Worker, behind MoveEngine

Why: GitHub Pages can't send the headers that threaded WebAssembly builds need.

Instead of: a TypeScript search of our own, Maia, or a personal model.

So: the WASM file is 7.3 MB (5.6 MB gzipped), not the planned 1.7 MB, so it has to load late.

Plan, built by S1. Entry: "Stockfish lite WASM, single thread, in a Web Worker, behind MoveEngine".

The repo is GPL-3.0-or-later

Why: the app ships Stockfish, which is GPL v3, and the repo had no licence.

Instead of: GPL-3.0-only.

So: copies taken after this reaches main stay GPL. This is a one-way door.

human kept -or-later and merged #61. Entry: "The repo is GPL-3.0-or-later because the app ships Stockfish".

Ship move counts, never game files

Why: TWIC is "free for personal use only". The Lichess database is CC0.

Instead of: committing PGN.

So: raw PGN stays in the git-ignored games/ folder. The plan calls this "a judgment call, not legal advice".

Plan. Covered in the GPL entry.

Two books, picked by the game's clock

Why: his slow and fast openings differ.

Instead of: one merged book.

So: base + 40 × increment of 900 s or more is slow. A line with fewer than 3 games falls back to the other book.

Plan, built by I1 and M1. Entry: "Two books, split by the game's clock".

Group by position, store only positions with 2 or more games

Why: transpositions merge, and a single-game line comes close to shipping that game.

Instead of: grouping by move order, or a floor of 1.

So: a line he played once leaves the book one move early. Books have a 3 MB cap each.

Sprint planner (agent default, waiting on you). Entries: "Book moves are grouped by position…" and "The book stores only positions reached by at least 2 of his games".

Load the engine and books when a vs-Liem game starts

Why: 7.3 MB of WASM and about 400 KB of books, and most visits may never play him.

Instead of: loading on page load.

So: the first game waits for the download ("Thinking…"). tests/build.test.ts keeps them out of the entry bundle.

Sprint planner, built by P1 and U3. Entry: "The engine and books load when a vs-Liem game starts…".

App green darkened to #047A4F

Why: white text on #059862 was 3.7:1, under 4.5:1.

Instead of: keeping it as a recorded exception.

So: Play, Sound-on and Start game all use the new green (5.38:1).

human picked the value. Built in wave fix 2. Entry: "App green darkened to #047A4F".

The Look: B's challenge card, A's margin note

Why: a card makes starting feel like a challenge, and a side note doesn't cover the board.

Instead of: look C, a table-heavy explorer.

So: flag red and gold accents, with red only for edges, never text.

human: "I like a mix between A and B". The planner chose which parts. Plan ## Look, and the draft linked above.

How a move of his flows

It merged each wave's slices into a wave branch, checked the wave in a browser, and opened one PR per wave into the plan branch. A reviewer then checked the final PR. Every slice was written test-first and reported medium confidence. Wave fix 1 reported high.
