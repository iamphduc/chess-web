★★★Post-game review against Liem

Plan report for post-game-review, merge target main. Final PR #73: review verdict pass in round 2.

After a game against Liem ends, you can press Review game. Stockfish at full strength checks every position, marks your inaccuracies, mistakes and blunders, and walks you through your 3 biggest drops. At each one you try to find a better move first, then see what you played, Stockfish's move and, when the position is in his book, what Liem played.

At a glance

Goal (from the plan): "After a vs-Liem game ends, the human can open a review that teaches." Before this plan, a game just ended with no feedback.

What shipped:

  • A Review game button after a finished vs-Liem game (checkmate, stalemate or time). It never shows in Two players or mid-game.
  • An analysis pass on his existing Stockfish worker: one search per position, 500 ms or depth 18, with a progress bar.
  • Grades at 10 / 20 / 30 win-% drop ?! ? ??, Lichess's per-move accuracy formula, and a game accuracy.
  • His book check: a move into his book is never flagged, and a note says where the game left his book.
  • A win % graph, a move list with marks, step buttons and arrow keys.
  • Up to 3 key moments with Find the better move, gold stars for found moves, and a bottom sheet on phones.

Key decisions

Red top edge: the human made it. Olive: an agent or plan default. Plan decisions are in the plan's ## Key decisions; the rest link to pod-docs/decisions.md by heading.

Grade levels stay 10 / 20 / 30

Why: stricter levels "would bury the big lessons" at club level.

Alternatives: Lichess's real 5 / 10 / 15, which "flags about twice as many moves".

Consequence: one edit to change (INACCURACY, MISTAKE, BLUNDER in grade.ts); the found rule uses INACCURACY too.

human answered an orchestrator queue entry. decisions.md: "Grade levels are 10/20/30 win-% points (chosen by the human)".

The review searches on his engine worker

Why: the app has one Stockfish worker, and it's idle after the game.

Alternatives: a second worker (more download and memory).

Consequence: his next turn aborts every review search first; a failed review search drops the engine so his next use loads a fresh one.

human chose "analyse after the game"; W1 recorded the worker entry. decisions.md: "The review searches on his engine worker".

500 ms or depth 18 a position

Why: the speed check: 380 ms a position on desktop, 473 ms in phone emulation (CPU 4×).

Alternatives: lower times; "scores get noisier below about 300 ms".

Consequence: about 30 s for a 40-move game on desktop, about 40 s on a slow phone (agent estimates). No real phone was tested.

agent default by W1. decisions.md: "Review searches stop at 500 ms or depth 18".

A move into his book is never flagged

Why: like Lichess's rule for master moves.

Alternatives: grade book moves like any other.

Consequence: book moves score 100 and read "he faced this in N games", not "Liem played this", because his book stores only his own moves.

human, wording corrected in the plan interview. Plan ## Key decisions.

Only vs-Liem, only the game that just ended

Why: nothing to save means no new storage.

Alternatives: saved games and a "Past games" list.

Consequence: reloading the page loses the review. Saved games is the next idea: pod-docs/ideas/saved-games.md.

human. Plan ## Key decisions.

The Look: A's coach card, B's graph, C's phone sheet

Why: the human's pick: "A plus the big graph point like B and also I like the idea of having the bottom sheet open like C in mobile view".

Alternatives: any one of the three directions alone.

Consequence: five grade tokens added to the vs-Liem palette; flag red only for edges.

human. Draft: review-look-directions.html.

Game accuracy is a plain average

Why: Lichess's volatility-weighted mean "is more work for a small change".

Alternatives: Lichess's mixed mean.

Consequence: book moves count as 100; accuracy shows "—" until every human move is graded.

plan default. Plan ## Key decisions.

A tried move is found if it loses under 10 %

Why: under the inaccuracy level.

Alternatives: only Stockfish's best counts.

Consequence: each try that isn't the best costs one extra search; exactly 10 isn't found.

plan default. Plan ## Key decisions.

The move list shows your move while you try

Why: "You already know what you played, and the mark only says it was a mistake."

Alternatives: hide that row or its mark while the moment is trying.

Consequence: the card says "Your move is hidden" while the list shows, for example, 2. g4 ??.

agent default, from the reviewer's round-1 finding. decisions.md: "The move list shows your move while you look for a better one".
