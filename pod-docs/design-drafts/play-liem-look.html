Liem opponent: three looks

Each look keeps the app's dark page, Quicksand and the Wheat board. They differ in how you set up a game against Liem and how his opening book shows while you play. Hover and pressed states are shown as static copies. Game counts are placeholders, not his real numbers. Pick A, B, C or a mix.

A

Notebook

Quiet and built in: the setup is one row of controls above the board, and the book is a margin note in the sidebar.

Memorable detail: the book note has an olive edge, the board's dark-square color. It turns red when the game leaves his book: "He's on his own from here."

Pre-game card

A real "challenge" moment: a card over the dimmed board where you pick your color and his strength, then the game starts.

Memorable detail: the colors of the Vietnamese flag. A red top edge on the card, and a gold star and gold strength steps for Liem. During the game, a small gold "Book" badge sits on his card.

Explorer

Memorable detail: a small Slow / Online switch on the table shows both books. It's the same split the bot uses to pick its moves.
